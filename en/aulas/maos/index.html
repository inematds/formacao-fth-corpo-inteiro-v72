Lesson 6 · 15 min

Hands: the problem that is still unsolved

The whole body walks, balances and imitates; when it is time to grasp, everything depends on a hand with few degrees of freedom and almost no touch. In this lesson you count the joints of the G1's hand in the model, compare it with two research hands, and measure a simulated grip, knowing what that number is and what it is not.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The G1's Dex3-1: 7 DoF per hand (thumb 3, index 2, middle 2)
  2. 2Torque declared in the MJCF and what it limits
  3. 3A simulated grip: contact force per finger
  4. 4Research hands (LEAP, Shadow) and the ones on the market, with sources

Goal: count the degrees of freedom of the G1's hand, compare it with other hands, and measure a simulated grip in the model.

02 · The hands you'll run into

Click each group.

1. On humanoids

Unitree Dex3-1: 7 DoF, optional tactile, included in the G1 EDU package (fact, unitree.com/g1).

In the model: both of the Menagerie G1's hands, with limits and torque declared.

2. From research

LEAP Hand: 16 DoF, one actuator per joint. Shadow Hand: 24 joints and 20 actuators, because some fingers have joints coupled by tendon.

Both models are in the Menagerie: just add the folder to the sparse-checkout.

3. From the market

Inspire RH56H1: 6 actuators and 11 joints, 260 tactile points, about US$ 4,500–5,780 resold (reported). Sharpa Wave: 22 DoF, over 1,000 sensors per fingertip, about US$ 20 thousand (reported, CES 2026).

The Optimus hand has no data verified by the course research. Hand prices vary by version and reseller; nothing here is a buying recommendation.

03 · Count the joints and squeeze a cylinder

mao.py counts the joints of each hand in the MJCF, reads the declared torque and closes the right hand around a fixed 4 cm cylinder, with the pelvis on the gantry. It reads each finger's normal contact force after 2 s.

Terminal · the G1 hand
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/corpo-inteiro && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
python3 kit/mao.py; echo "saída: $?"

7 degrees of freedom per hand, as in the Dex3-1 spec sheet. The forces come from the model: the cylinder is fixed, the PD pushes up to the torque limit, and the middle finger grips close to the joint. This is not the robot's grip force, which Unitree doesn't publish on the page I read.

The G1's scene.xml is the 29-DoF version, without hands. For the hands, use unitree_g1/scene_with_hands.xml.

04 · Two research hands to compare

Adds the LEAP Hand and Shadow Hand folders to your Menagerie (sparse-checkout add, without downloading the rest) and counts the joints.

Terminal · LEAP and Shadow
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/mujoco_menagerie && git sparse-checkout add leap_hand shadow_hand
cd $F/corpo-inteiro && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
for h in leap_hand/right_hand.xml shadow_hand/right_hand.xml; do python3 kit/mao.py --modelo $h | tail -1 | sed "s#^#$h: #"; done
Example from the repository
leap_hand/right_hand.xml: mão avulsa: 16 GDL · 16 atuadores
shadow_hand/right_hand.xml: mão avulsa: 24 GDL · 20 atuadores

On the Shadow, 24 joints for 20 actuators: four joints follow others through coupling. More DoF is not the same as more motors.
