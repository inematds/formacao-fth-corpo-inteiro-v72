Lesson 4 · 15 min

A policy that imitates any motion

Having the motion in the robot's format is not the same as making it perform it. If you send only the angles to the motors, it imitates for a second and falls: nobody is handling balance. In this lesson you play the boxing in MuJoCo with and without a gantry, measure each joint's error, and see what BeyondMimic, GMT and SONIC solve.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  2. 2Tracking error in degrees, per joint
  3. 3Why following angles doesn't balance the robot
  4. 4BeyondMimic (now in mjlab), GMT and SONIC: what each one does

02 · Who solves motion tracking in 2026

Click on each one. All of them train on a GPU; here they are shown, not run.

1. BeyondMimic

Tracks dynamic motions (spinning, running, cartwheel) and uses a guided diffusion policy for tasks without specific training (arXiv 2508.08241, Aug/2025). The whole_body_tracking repository pins Isaac Lab 2.1.0.

As of 10/15/2026, that repository's README says it has been retired: BeyondMimic tracking continues in mjlab, in the Mjlab-Tracking-Flat-Unitree-G1 task.

2. GMT

General tracking with adaptive sampling (easy × hard motions) and a mixture-of-experts layer (arXiv 2506.14770, Jun/2025). Reported by the course research, based on the paper's abstract.

3. SONIC

From NVIDIA: 42M parameters, trained on more than 100M frames (about 700 h). It has three public checkpoints for the G1 (original, low latency, and v1.1 from 07/23/2026), 64-dimension tokens, and a 50 Hz controller.

Deployment in C++ and TensorRT; code under Apache-2.0, weights under the NVIDIA Open Model License (commercial use with attribution).

FormatGMR exports CSV for BeyondMimic, whose imitation pipeline mjlab adopted. Your .npz from lesson 3 is the same data in the kit's own format.

03 · Replay with the pelvis on the gantry

The joints receive the reference at each 2 ms step; the base is placed back at the reference pose. The error is the difference between the measured angle and the reference angle at the next step.

Example from the repository
movimento: boxe_g1.npz · 1200 quadros a 120 Hz · 10.0 s · modo portico
passos de física: 4995 (2 ms cada)
erro médio de junta: 0.3° · pior junta: right_shoulder_roll_joint (1.9°)
queda: não
saída: 0

Average error of 0.3°: with the body supported, the PD handles it. The right shoulder, which moves the most in boxing, is the worst.

movimento: boxe_g1.npz · 1200 quadros a 120 Hz · 10.0 s · modo livre
passos de física: 839 (2 ms cada)
erro médio de junta: 0.5° · pior junta: right_shoulder_roll_joint (2.8°)
queda: sim, em t = 1.68 s
saída: 0

The same replay, unsupported: it falls at 1.68 s with the joint error still small. Following the angles is not balancing. That is what the tracking policy learns.
