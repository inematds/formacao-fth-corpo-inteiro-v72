Lesson 1 · 15 min

From walking to doing: whole-body control

Walking is just the start. To pick up a box, wave, or obey a VLA, the humanoid has to coordinate legs, torso, and arms at the same time, without falling. In this lesson you set up the course workbench and, in a simulation loop, count the three layers that make this happen: who decides, who coordinates the body, and who moves each joint.

  3. 3The 2025–26 paradigm: a policy that tracks human motion
  4. 4The workbench in ~/fth reused from course 0

02 · What changed from 2024 to 2026

Click each step.

1. Before

Humanoid control was built from hand-written blocks: a step planner, the foot's trajectory in the air, the support leg, and balance via ZMP or MPC (model predictive control).

It works and it is interpretable, but every new skill needs new engineering. Lesson 6 of FTH course 0 showed the center of mass and the support polygon behind it.

2. 2025–26

The open path became: human motion capture or video → retargeting (GMR) → a motion tracking policy trained with PPO across thousands of simulations (BeyondMimic, GMT, SONIC) → sim2sim → robot.

One policy imitates any reference motion and serves as the base for teleoperation and for the VLA. Figure described the same thing in Helix 02: a 10M-parameter controller at 1 kHz, trained on more than 1.000 h of human motion, which replaced 109.504 lines of C++.

CaveatMPC × RL: the research for this course found no independent quantitative comparison in 2026. MPC remains strong where a guarantee is required; RL is the dominant practice in open repositories.

3. Decoupled

A widely used middle ground: RL on the legs (walking and balancing) and IK on the arms (reaching). This is the decoupled WBC from the GR00T-WholeBodyControl repository, used under GR00T N1.5 and N1.6.

This course's kit follows that split: legs in the model's "stand" pose, arms via IK.

04 · Count the layers in a 2-second loop

hierarquia.py runs the G1 standing for 2 s. Every 200 ms (task) it switches the right-hand target; every 20 ms (S0) it redoes the arm IK; every 2 ms it takes one physics step with the motors' PD.

1000 PD steps for 100 S0 decisions and 10 task decisions. The hand ends less than 1 cm from the last target and the robot is still standing.

ERRO: S0 (30 Hz) e tarefa (5 Hz) precisam dividir o passo de 500 Hz.
saída: 1

The bottom loop sets the clock: the upper layers run every N steps. 30 Hz does not fit into 500 Hz a whole number of times. Use divisors (50, 100, 250 Hz).

S0 corpo inteiro (IK)    :  200 chamadas (100 Hz)

Twice as many body decisions in the same time. Helix 02 takes this to the extreme: the learned S0 runs at 1 kHz.
